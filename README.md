A web-based platform dedicated to showcasing and advertising small, local township (Kasi) businesses. 
This initiative aims to give local entrepreneurs the visibility they need to connect with customers, grow their brands, and access support.


## 🌟 About the Project

In many local communities, small businesses possess immense potential and unique offerings but often struggle with digital visibility and marketing reach. **Kasi Business** acts as a centralized directory and showcase page where these enterprises can be easily discovered by potential clients and supporters.


## 🛠️ Built With

* **HTML5** – Core structure and markup
* **CSS3** – Styling and responsive layout design
* **GitHub Pages** – Hosting and deployment


## 🚀 Features

* **Local Business Showcase:** A clean interface highlighting various small businesses and their services.
* **Accessibility:** Designed to be lightweight and fast-loading for mobile and desktop users.
* **Community-Centric:** Built with the goal of uplifting local entrepreneurs and driving community-led economic growth.
